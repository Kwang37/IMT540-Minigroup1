    Reflection 1 · Before the Gallery

    Look at your arrangement before seeing anyone else’s. A sentence or two is enough.

    Need help getting started?

    Look at your arrangement. Pick one detail you can point to and explain what you wanted it to do.

    • Which squares did you put close together or far apart? Why?
    • Did you line up any edges, overlap squares, or turn one? What effect did you want?
    • Where did you leave empty space? What does it make you notice?
